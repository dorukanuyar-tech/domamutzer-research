# Evidence Ledger

Domamutzer keeps development failures visible because they determine what can be claimed.

## GEMSEC Deezer v2.0 — AMBER

External historical friendship proxy. Mean top-K lift was positive across three country networks, but only Hungary had a confidence interval wholly above zero.

## GitHub social v2.1 — RED

Fresh attributed social-network holdout. The frozen added-feature model did not beat the matched conventional baseline on the primary NDCG@10 metric.

## Twitch v2.3 — RED

Six language networks, all reported. Macro paired NDCG@10 delta was `-0.000828`, pooled 95% CI crossed zero, and only PTBR/RU had positive mean deltas.

## Interpretation

These results reject the earlier hypothesis that the same symmetric reciprocal/rarity feature family should be switched on broadly across unrelated social graphs. They do **not** test the final product objective because they lack recommendation impressions, two directional acceptance labels, continuation outcomes, and safety outcomes.

The current v4 architecture was designed after these results and should be judged on buyer-controlled directional replay/shadow/A-B evidence rather than by re-opening and retuning the old final holdouts.
