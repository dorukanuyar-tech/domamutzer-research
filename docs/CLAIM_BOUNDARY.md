# Claim Boundary

Domamutzer does **not** currently claim:

- superiority over Meta, TikTok, Snap or another production recommender,
- causal improvement in friendship formation,
- statistically significant positive lift across all public networks,
- validation of directional mutual acceptance from undirected friendship data.

Supported statements:

- the private engine implements a versioned people-discovery reranking/evaluation stack,
- public benchmark history includes both positive and negative results,
- v3 uses a baseline-first safe residual policy motivated by those holdout failures,
- the engine supports a true directional reciprocal objective when a partner supplies directional labels.
